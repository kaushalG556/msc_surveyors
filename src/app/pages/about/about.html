ABOUT THE COMPANY

Who We Are

Marine Surveyors and Consultants is an independent organization specializing in comprehensive vessel, cargo and maritime inspections. With years of industry experience, we ensure accurate reports, regulatory compliance and operational safety throughout the maritime logistics chain.

Our team of highly qualified surveyors, naval architects and marine consultants provides objective and technically sound assessments compliant with international standards.

Our Commitment

To support safe navigation, protect cargo integrity, ensure regulatory compliance and safeguard the marine environment by delivering transparent, impartial and technically sound assessments.
